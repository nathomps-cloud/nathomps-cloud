Step 9 — capture system
Core rule: No idea lives only in your head. Your brain is for thinking. Your systems are for storage. Capture everything. Sort nothing in the moment.
Voice inbox
  • One app only — Recorder or Whisper
  • Single gesture trigger via Good Lock
  • No editing on capture — just dump
  • Process weekly not daily
Quick notes
  • One inbox: Keep or Obsidian
  • No sorting at capture time
  • Dump first, organize weekly
  • Lock screen shortcut if possible
Screenshots
  • Double-tap back = instant capture
  • Weekly review cadence
  • Archive or act — nothing sits idle
  • Use Samsung Gallery auto-organize
Bookmarks
  • One destination: Raindrop or Pocket
  • No reading inside browser
  • Save now → read in batches later
  • Weekly clear-out session
Checklist
Pick and commit to one voice memo app
Pick and commit to one quick-note inbox
Assign voice app to a Good Lock gesture shortcut
Set double-tap back → screenshot in RegiStar
Install Raindrop or Pocket
Schedule weekly capture review (Sundays work)
