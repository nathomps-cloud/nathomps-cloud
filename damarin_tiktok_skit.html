The Concept

This text appears instantly on screen, no delay. Use bold white text, black stroke. Font: Impact or heavy sans. Place in upper third.

CapCut Edit Workflow
Step-by-Step in CapCut
Do it in this order to avoid rework
1
Import clips in order
Add clips 1–5 in script order. Don't trim yet — just get them on the timeline.
2
Add the sound first
Drop your audio track before you start cutting. Everything cuts to the music — not the other way around.
3
Trim to beat hits
Use CapCut's waveform view. Cut on the kick drum or a beat hit. Shot 3 (the best clip) should START exactly on a big beat hit.
4
Add text overlays
Use each overlay from the Script tab. Font: Montserrat ExtraBold or Impact. Color: white with black stroke (3–4px). Animate: "Pop" or "Typewriter."
5
Transitions
Keep it simple: Flash or Zoom transition between Shot 2 → 3 for maximum impact. All others: straight cut (no transition).
6
Color grade
Apply CapCut filter: "Moody" or "Cinema" at 40–60% intensity. Boosts contrast and makes clips look more professional instantly.
7
Export & post
Export at 1080p. Post directly from CapCut to TikTok. Do NOT save → upload separately — that gets flagged as repost and suppressed.
Viral Mechanics Checklist
Every box = more reach
Hook in frame 1: text visible within 0.5 seconds of the video starting
No dead air: every clip has either motion, text, or audio change — never flat
Loop potential: ending clips back into beginning naturally — boosts watch-through rate
Faces visible: TikTok's algorithm favors face-forward clips — make sure Damarin's face is clear in Shot 3
Under 45 seconds: high rewatch rate at this length kills it in the algorithm
Comment bait in last 3 seconds: the "drop a 🔥" text from the Script tab triggers engagement at peak watch-through
